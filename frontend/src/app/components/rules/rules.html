Game Rules

Welcome to RegexRiddle. Read before you start playing.

The Objective

RegexRiddle is a platform designed to test your knowledge of Regular Expressions (RegEx). You can play as a Solver, trying to crack existing puzzles, or as a Creator, designing challenges to stump other players.

How to Solve

  • 1. Browse the Home page and select a challenge. You must be logged in to play.
  • 2. Read the Challenge Briefing. The creator will provide one public string that matches the regex, and one that does not.
  • 3. Write a Regular Expression that you think satisfies the logic.
  • 4. When you submit, your regex will be tested against up to 20 hidden strings. To win, your regex must match all positive strings and reject all negative strings.
